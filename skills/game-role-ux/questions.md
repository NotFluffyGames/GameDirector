# UX: questions

Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer. Push a vague answer until a capture or a log could contradict it.

## Ideation (`north-star`)

1. Who is the target player: genre experience and skill? (Recorded as `player`.)
2. Which input devices, and which is primary? (Recorded as `input`.)
3. Where and for how long is a session played? (Recorded as `session`.)
4. Which access needs must the minimum meet: remapping, subtitles, colour-safe cues, shake and flash toggles, assist options? (Recorded as `access`.)
5. What must a new player understand in the first minute? (Recorded as `statement`.)
6. Which games show a first minute or a HUD we borrow from, and what do we take? (Recorded as `references`.)

## Brainstorm (`challenge`)

- What must the player perceive to use this, and how critical is it?
- Through which channel does it arrive: world, HUD, audio or haptic, and is there a second for those who miss the first?
- Is it taught by doing, where, and what shows a failed attempt what went wrong?
- Which input does it use, and does it conflict with another verb or device?
- What does it add to the HUD, and what competes with it for attention?
- Which friction here is the challenge, and which is not?
