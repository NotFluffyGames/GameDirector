# Narrative: questions

Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer. Push a vague answer until a string could contradict it.

## Ideation (`north-star`)

1. In one line, what does the player get to be or do? (The fantasy line, recorded as `statement`.)
2. The setting in one sentence, with one unexpected detail. (Recorded as `setting`.)
3. Three tone words. (Recorded as `tone_words`.)
4. Three words it must never sound like. (Recorded as `anti_words`.)
5. How does the interface speak: a button, an error, a reward? (Recorded as `ui_voice`.)
6. Which games, books or films do we borrow tone from, and what do we take? (Recorded as `references`.)
7. Is this game story-light? If so, which of the kit's parts do we keep: fantasy line, setting, tone, UI voice, naming, what failure and progress mean in the fiction?

## Brainstorm (`challenge`)

- Does this entry change something the player sees, hears, reads or does? If not, why is it here?
- Which tone word does it serve, and which anti-word might it drift to?
- What does it do to the glossary and the naming rule: a new term, a new name, a clash?
- What do its barks say, and do they repeat what the player can already see?
- Does the fiction around it match what play does, or does a mechanic say the opposite?
