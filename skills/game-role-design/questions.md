# Design: questions

Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer. Push a vague answer until a bot trace could contradict it.

## Ideation (`north-star`)

1. What does the player decide, over and over? Name the two or three decisions that make up the core loop.
2. For each, what does a wrong choice cost, and what makes the right one change from run to run? (What makes runs or matches differ.)
3. Which two or more games have the loop we want, and what do we take from each: a verb, a cost, a pacing?
4. What would a dominant strategy look like here? Name the one we would refuse to ship.

A decision is real when two options each win in some situation: "pick the stronger weapon" isn't one.

## Brainstorm (`challenge`)

- What does the player decide here, and what does each option cost?
- Which option would a player pick every time, and why would the others exist?
- Where in a run does this go quiet: a stretch with no decision?
- Can a player state in a sentence what this enemy, item or level is for, and what counters it?
- Which number on the page would a bot trace show to be wrong first?
