# Creative: questions

Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer. Push a vague answer until a capture could contradict it.

## Ideation (`north-star`)

Ask last, after the other roles' north stars. Start from the `landscape` page and the person's picks from the opening of ideation (comps that count as "us", anchors to break, hook); propose answers from them, and skip a question they already settle.

1. What would most games of this kind do, in the first minute and over a whole run? (The genre default.)
2. Where does ours depart? Name the one or two places the player would feel it. (The departure.)
3. Which two or more games, films or artworks show the departure, and what do you take from each?
4. Which things could polish never remove without it becoming something else? (Candidate invariants; cut to two to five.)

An invariant is a thing a capture or a play session can confirm or deny: "enemies never telegraph with a red outline" passes, "feels fresh" doesn't.

## Brainstorm (`challenge`)

- What would most games of this kind do at this decision?
- Which pillar does the answer serve, and which invariant would it put at risk?
- If we cut this, would a player of the genre default notice?
- What does the answer share with its closest reference, and what is the deliberate difference?
