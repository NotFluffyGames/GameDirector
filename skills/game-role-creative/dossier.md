# Creative: dossier section

File: `docs/game/research/refs/<id>/creative.md`. Short; facts and sources, no summary of the game.

## Required

- `how_studied`: `played`, `watched`, `read` or `not-assessed` with the reason.
- **Hook**: one sentence on what the reference does that its genre default doesn't, with the evidence (a quote, a number, a capture).
- **Overdone**: at least two conventions, each with a source (a review, a community thread or a capture).
- **Room**: what this game takes and what it avoids, each tied to this game's north star or an invariant.
- **Sources**: a list; every claim above points to one.

## Audit rules

The creative `audit` flags a finding, anchored to the rule, when:

- `sourceless`: a claim has no source;
- `generic`: a line could describe a different game ("polished", "great art style");
- `missing`: a required field, number or image is absent and not marked `not-assessed` with a reason.
