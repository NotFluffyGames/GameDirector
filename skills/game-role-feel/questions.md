# Feel: questions

Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer. Push a vague answer until a strip could contradict it.

## Ideation (`north-star`)

Ask per core action, the verbs the player repeats most.

1. Which one or two adjectives should this action feel like? ("Heavy and crunchy", not "good".)
2. Which one to three clips show it, and at what timestamp does the moment happen?
3. What should it never feel like? (Recorded as `never`.)
4. Does a bigger version of the action feel bigger, and how: more layers, longer, louder, more shake?

## Brainstorm (`challenge`)

- Which core action does this topic touch, and what adjective is it held to?
- What does the player see within how many frames of pressing the button?
- Which feedback layers fire (animation, effect, sound, camera, hit pause, knockback, haptic), and which one carries the signal?
- When it happens ten times in a second, does it still read or does it turn to noise?
- Which window forgives a late or early input, and how wide is it in frames?
