# Art: questions

Ask one at a time, with your recommended answer. Push a vague answer until a capture could contradict it.

## Ideation (`north-star`)

1. In one line, what should the game look like? (The slogan.)
2. Which three to five rules does every image obey, and what does each rule out? ("Big silhouettes; no texture noise.")
3. Which games, films or artworks do we draw from, and what do we take from each: shape, palette, lighting, material, UI?
4. Show two or three divergent directions, each with its slogan, rules and references. Which one, and why did the others lose?
5. Which colours are reserved for the player, threats, interactables and pickups? (Recorded as `reserved`.)

## Brainstorm (`challenge`)

- Which colour role and value band does this occupy?
- Does its silhouette read at thumbnail size, in grayscale, beside the nearest other piece?
- What does the eye see first, second, third on the screen it appears in?
- Which bible rule does it test, and which asset would break that rule first?
- Is any of it sourced or generated, and what normalises it to the bible?
