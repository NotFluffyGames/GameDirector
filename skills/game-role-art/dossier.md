# Art: dossier section

File: `docs/game/research/refs/<id>/art.md`, with saved shots under `docs/game/research/refs/<id>/art/`. Short; facts and sources.

## Required

- `how_studied`: `played`, `watched` (frames), `read` or `not-assessed` with the reason.
- **Shots**: at least two saved image paths (a play moment and the UI).
- **Palette**: the colours as hex estimated by eye from the shots and their `derive` copies, marked "estimated", each with its role.
- **Shape and value**: silhouette language and value bands per layer, from the shots.
- **UI layout**: where the HUD sits and what it holds.
- **Take / avoid**: each tied to this game's slogan or a rule-pillar.
- **Sources**: a list; every claim points to one.

## Audit rules

The creative `audit` flags a finding, anchored to the rule, when:

- `sourceless`: a claim has no source;
- `generic`: a line could describe a different game ("great art style", "colourful");
- `missing`: an image, a hex palette or a required field is absent and not marked `not-assessed` with a reason.
