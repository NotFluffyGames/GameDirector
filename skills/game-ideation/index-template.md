# Wiki home page template

Page template for the wiki's home page (id `index`, area Vision) in the docs place; setup creates it from this. Keep the `##` headings exactly, one per area: the areas in `docs/game/wiki.json` fill them. Brainstorm adds a line under the right heading for every page it creates, linking the page.

```markdown
# <Game title> wiki

The agent-facing description of the game: current truth only. Why things changed lives in the ADRs each page links to.

## Vision

- [Vision](<link to the vision page>): pitch, audience, pillars, core loop, target feeling, scope
- [Vertical slice](<link to the slice page>): what the slice contains, what it must prove, its quality bar
- [Directions considered](<link to the concepts page>): the directions compared and why this one

## Mechanics

## Controls

## Systems

## Content

## Modes and sessions

## Screens and onboarding

## Feel

## Art

## Audio

## Narrative and world
```
